Answers to your questions

Can't find what you need? Join the beta and ask us directly.

What kinds of investments can I track?

Brokerage, retirement (401(k), IRA) and crypto accounts. Stocks, ETFs, mutual funds and bonds are supported, and you can add other assets by hand or by CSV.

Does Net Worth give insights on my portfolio?

Yes. The AI advisor explains performance, allocation and concentration risk in plain language, based on your connected data. Insights are educational, not personalized advice.

Can Net Worth trade or move my money?

Not during the beta. Connections are read-only. Investing products are on our roadmap and would launch with licensed partners.

How much money do I need to get started?

None. Tracking is free during the beta, whatever your balance.

Is my data secure?

Connections are read-only and encrypted, and we never store your brokerage passwords.
