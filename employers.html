Answers for employers

Want to talk it through? Book a call with the founders.

How much does it cost?

Per-employee, per-month pricing. Design partners get pilot pricing locked for the first year. Book a demo for a quote.

Can we see our employees' financial data?

No. You get aggregate, anonymized engagement and outcome reporting. Individual data stays private to each employee.

How long does rollout take?

Pilots can launch in a few weeks. We handle launch emails, onboarding sessions and reminders with your HR team.

Do you integrate with our HR or benefits platform?

Eligibility file and SSO support are planned for pilots. Tell us your stack and we'll prioritize it.

Is this financial advice?

The AI provides educational insights. Credentialed planner access is on our roadmap and would be offered through licensed partners.
