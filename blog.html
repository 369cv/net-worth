Personal finance

Money stress is everywhere. Here's what actually helps

10.28.2026

Surveys consistently rank money as a leading source of stress. The fix usually isn't a perfect budget: it's visibility. Knowing exactly where you stand turns a vague worry into a list of decisions.

Start with three numbers: what you own, what you owe, and what you spend in a typical month. Everything else builds on those.

Personal finance

Planning when the economy feels stuck

10.20.2026

When headlines are confusing, plans that depend on predicting markets break first. Plans built on savings rate, emergency cushion and time horizon don't.

A forecast you can adjust (what if rates stay high? what if I take a career break?) beats a single guess.

For HR leaders

Designing a benefits strategy around financial wellness

10.14.2026

Most benefit stacks already include a 401(k) match, an HSA and an EAP, yet participation is often low because employees don't understand them.

A financial wellness layer connects those benefits to each person's real situation, so the money you already spend actually gets used.

For HR leaders

Redefining wellness benefits: a holistic approach to employee well-being

10.07.2026

Physical and mental health programs are now standard. Financial health is the missing third leg, and it affects the other two.

Treat it as part of one well-being program: shared goals, shared measurement, and a single place employees go for help.

For HR leaders

How buy-now-pay-later is changing employees' finances

09.30.2026

Buy-now-pay-later makes small purchases feel free, but stacked plans can quietly eat a paycheck. Because many plans don't show up the way card debt does, people often underestimate them.

Tools that surface every upcoming installment in one calendar help employees see the real total before it becomes a problem.
