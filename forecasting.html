Answers to your questions

Can't find what you need? Join the beta and ask us directly.

How does the forecast work?

It projects your net worth from your current balances, income, savings rate and assumed market returns. You can change any assumption.

Can I model different scenarios?

Yes. Add milestones like a home purchase or a career break and compare paths side by side.

Are projections guaranteed?

No. Forecasts are hypothetical estimates for planning and education, not guarantees of future results.

Does it account for inflation?

Yes. You can view results in today's dollars or future dollars.
