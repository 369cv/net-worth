Questions, answered

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. Account connections are read-only, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never store your bank passwords.

When can I get access?

We are in private beta and invite people from the waitlist every week, starting with the web app. iOS follows, then Android.

How much does Net Worth cost?

Free for everyone during the beta. Paid plans arrive after launch, and beta members get founding-member pricing.

Which banks and brokerages are supported?

Thousands of US banks, credit cards, brokerages, retirement accounts, loans and crypto exchanges. You can also add assets like real estate manually.

Is the AI advisor giving financial advice?

The AI provides educational insights based on your data. It isn't a substitute for a licensed professional. Please read our disclosures.

Can I share with my partner but keep some accounts private?

Yes. You choose which accounts are shared, account by account.

Can I export or delete my data?

Anytime. Export to CSV or delete your account and all data from Settings.
