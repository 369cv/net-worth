Answers to your questions

Can't find what you need? Join the beta and ask us directly.

When can I get access?

We're in private beta and invite people from the waitlist every week, starting with the web app. iOS follows, then Android.

Is my financial data secure?

Account connections are read-only through a regulated aggregation partner. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never see or store your bank passwords.

How much does Net Worth cost?

It's free for everyone during the beta. Paid plans arrive after launch, and beta members keep founding-member pricing.

Which banks and brokerages are supported?

Thousands of US banks, cards, brokerages, retirement accounts, loans and crypto exchanges. You can also add assets like real estate by hand.

Is the AI advisor giving financial advice?

No. The AI provides educational insights based on your data and isn't a substitute for a licensed professional.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes, at any time. Export to CSV or delete your account and all of your data from Settings.
